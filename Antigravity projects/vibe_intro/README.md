# Celestial Horizon — Night Sky & Meteor Shower

An interactive night sky and meteor shower experience built with **p5.js** and the **Web Audio API**.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Dynamic Cosmic Sky**: Deep vertical space gradients with multi-octave procedural nebula clouds rendered with Perlin noise.
- **Layered Star Field**: Multi-layer depth with realistic spectral star temperatures (blue giants, golden dwarfs, white stars, amber stars), natural scintillation/twinkling, diffraction cross-spikes, and parallax mouse motion.
- **Physics-based Meteor Shower**:
  - Radiant trajectories with variable entry velocity and ionized gas wake trails.
  - Rare **bolide fireballs** with glowing auras that explode into showers of sparkling embers.
  - Floating vapor trail sparks and smoke decay.
- **Interactive Controls**:
  - **Click & Drag**: Aim and throw custom shooting stars across the sky with variable speed.
  - **Stardust Cursor**: Interactive glowing celestial dust follows pointer movement.
  - **Meteor Burst**: Trigger coordinated multi-meteor volleys.
  - **Observatory HUD**: Adjust meteor frequency (from calm to full meteor storm), star density, and nebula visibility.
  - **Atmospheric Silhouette**: Toggle between pure deep space and a pine forest horizon silhouette.
  - **Procedural Audio Synth**: Zero-dependency Web Audio API synthesizer featuring deep space drone pads, crystalline chime sweeps for shooting stars, and fireball detonation rumbles.
