Nosu

2025

This garment draws inspiration from the rich cultural heritage of the Nosu (Yi) minority group, reinterpreting its traditional cultural essence through a contemporary lens. I intentionally break free from conventional ethnic costume silhouettes, deconstructing and reimagining classic structural forms to craft fluid proportions that bridge heritage and avant-garde design.

Serial Killer

2025

Serial Killer is a fashion collection that reimagines the killer as something internal: the nightmares that repeatedly return to haunt us. These nightmares emerge from deeply rooted fears, unresolved regrets, guilt, and the memories for which we still seek forgiveness. Rather than depicting literal violence, the collection explores the quiet psychological tension of being pursued by one’s own mind. Delicate lace, translucent layers, distorted silhouettes, and unusual material combinations create a dreamy yet unsettling atmosphere, where softness and unease coexist. The body appears partially concealed and partially exposed, suspended between reality and dream, beauty and fear, vulnerability and protection.

Window

2024

This collection is inspired by the classical gardens of Suzhou, particularly their intricate windows and the relationship they create between positive and negative space. Rather than functioning simply as architectural decoration, each window frames the landscape beyond it, transforming plants, rocks, light, and movement into part of its composition. Drawing from this practice of constructed and borrowed scenery, the garments use cutouts, layered structures, and shifting silhouettes to frame the body and its surroundings. Empty space becomes an active design element, allowing what lies behind the garment to continuously reshape its visual form.
