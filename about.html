Yixi Tang

Yixi Tang is a Chicago-based interdisciplinary artist and designer working across fashion, creative technology, and interactive media. Their practice combines garment construction, material experimentation, sensor-based systems, computer vision, and generative AI to explore how technology reshapes the body, identity, and physical experience.

Yixi is completing a BFA in Fashion and Art & Technology at the School of the Art Institute of Chicago (SAIC), expected in 2026. Their work spans bio-responsive wearables, environmental data visualization, experimental garments, and computational media, bringing digital systems into material and embodied forms.
