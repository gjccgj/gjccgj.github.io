Yixi Tang

Yixi Tang is now a Chicago-based interdisciplinary fashion and art-tech artist whose work explores the intersection between physical garment design and virtual digital experiences through experimental materials, interactive media, and computational tools. Drawing inspi- ration from digital culture, hybrid identity, and the blurring boundary between real and virtual spaces, they create innovative, cross-disciplinary works that reimagine fashion as both physical craft and immersive digital expression.

Yixi Tang is currently pursuing the studies at the School of the Art Institute of Chicago(SAIC). Their creative projects have been presented in student exhibitions and experimental showcases at SAIC.
